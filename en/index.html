Projects

Work carried out during my studies, alongside my internships.

Tokamak module · CEA Cadarache (IRFM) · February 2026 · team of 4

Tokamak pre-design with the D0FUS systems code

Exploring a reactor's (R0, a) design space under MHD stability constraints (Greenwald, Troyon and kink limits) and radial build constraints.

  • Influence of plasma temperature, peak magnetic field, fusion power and confinement factor H.
  • Comparison of NbTi, Nb3Sn and REBCO superconductors from 6 to 20 T: only REBCO remains both feasible and stable at 20 T.
  • Choice of coil steel (316L, N50H) and of bucking vs wedging mechanical configurations.

Tokamak module · IRFM × GOLEM tokamak (Prague) · February 2026 · pair project

Runaway electrons in the GOLEM tokamak

A week of remotely operated experiments on the GOLEM tokamak (Czech Technical University in Prague), presented at IRFM.

  • Scans in gas pressure, toroidal field and vessel cleanliness.
  • Hard X-ray detection (Timepix3, scintillators) and runaway-electron counting by Gaussian fitting in Python.
  • The toroidal field affects runaway-electron confinement rather than their generation; fewer electrons are detected at higher gas pressure.

T7004T – Surface Engineering · Luleå University of Technology · 2023 · team lab work

Identifying metallic coatings on unknown substrates

Methods: optical and stereo microscopy, SEM-EDS (composition and thickness), Vickers hardness, roughness by 3D interferometry, friction wear testing.

  • Identified TiN coatings and a nitrided layer on Ti6Al4V and FeCr substrates.
  • Clear hardness improvement for some samples.
  • Correlation between microstructure, roughness and tribological behaviour.

T7002T – Materials Modeling · Luleå University of Technology · 2023 · team course project

Multiscale modelling of wood-fibre composites

Approach: analysis of the cell-wall layers (P, S1, S2, S3), rule of mixtures, concentric cylinder assembly (CCA) model, classical laminate theory, finite-element simulations in ANSYS Mechanical.

  • Computed the elastic constants of the fibres and of wood/epoxy composites (aligned and random fibres).
  • Showed the dominant role of the S2 layer in stiffness.
  • Compared Young's moduli, shear moduli and Poisson's ratios.

PPEF Master's · Université de Lorraine · 2025

Numerical methods for plasma physics

Three successive numerical projects applied to turbulence and transport in magnetised plasmas.

  • Bispectral analysis to detect nonlinear wave coupling.
  • Solving the Hasegawa–Mima equation with a pseudo-spectral method.
  • High-order advection schemes: numerical dispersion and stability.
